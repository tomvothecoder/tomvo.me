# Curated Visual References for Strength Coaching Website

**Design direction:** Editorial minimalism with subtle scientific influence.

These are **archived or third-party screenshots of existing websites**, not new mockups and not necessarily the current live versions. Use them for visual reference only. Do not reproduce brand assets, images, layouts, text, or proprietary graphics in the finished site.

## Priority and interpretation

The first two are the primary visual identity references. The other three supply coaching UX, strength-specific content cues, and restraint in scientific presentation. Do not average all five aesthetics together.

### 01. Forner Studio — Primary editorial direction
**File:** `01-forner-editorial.jpg`  
**Screenshot:** https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/sites_of_the_day/2022/08/forner-interior-2.jpg  
**Original/credit:** https://www.awwwards.com/sites/forner-studio  
**Current website:** https://forner.studio/  
**Borrow:** Warm off-white canvas, generous negative space, editorial alignment, image/text composition, quiet premium feel.  
**Avoid:** Brand-specific content, extreme text clipping, decorative script, or fashion-studio art direction unrelated to lifting.

### 02. Linear — Typography and UI precision
**File:** `02-linear-typography.avif`  
**Screenshot:** https://cdn.sanity.io/images/jqi5lycx/production/c58b4808b00a69473124530e1a700b93a27b524a-1560x975.avif?auto=format&fit=max&w=1600  
**Original/credit:** https://www.khod.io/resource-center/articles/ai-website-examples  
**Current website:** https://linear.app/  
**Borrow:** Concise copy, display hierarchy, measured spacing, disciplined alignment, precise navigation/CTA details.  
**Avoid:** Dark-mode as default, software-product dashboard visuals, tiny low-contrast body text.

### 03. Future — Coaching proposition and conversion
**File:** `03-future-coaching.png`  
**Screenshot:** https://futureworks.payloadcms.app/media/future-cbmmVxAs9nFaeHgMiCWb1-1707900544863.png  
**Original/credit:** https://future.works/case-studies/future/  
**Current website:** https://future.co/  
**Borrow:** Human-centered photography, understandable coaching proposition, clean CTA, welcoming presentation.  
**Avoid:** Exact marketing copy, lifestyle-only imagery, overlaying text on busy photos without contrast, subscription claims not offered by our site.

### 04. JuggernautAI — Strength-specific credibility (secondary only)
**File:** `04-juggernaut-strength.webp`  
**Screenshot:** https://cdn-image.creati.ai/ai-tools/product-image/juggernautai-app.webp  
**Original/credit:** https://creati.ai/es/ai-tools/juggernautai-app/  
**Current website:** https://www.juggernautai.app/  
**Borrow:** Recognizable barbell/powerlifting subject matter; show serious training without excluding new lifters.  
**Avoid:** Dark blue/red brand colors, heavy badges, exaggerated athletic marketing, AI-related claims.

### 05. IntervalCoach — Scientific visualization (secondary only)
**File:** `05-intervalcoach-analytics.png`  
**Screenshot:** https://www.intervalcoach.app/_next/image?dpl=dpl_5u7VVVxKR7uMQucrpoDeffjNLCj3&q=75&url=%2Fscreenshots%2Fanalytics-light.png&w=1920  
**Original/credit:** https://www.intervalcoach.app/  
**Borrow:** Clear line charts, readable axes, limited color accents, restrained data display.  
**Avoid:** Turning a marketing website into an analytics dashboard, showing unsupported scientific metrics, implying cycling data are lifting outcomes.

### Additional content-only reference
**Barbell Medicine:** https://www.barbellmedicine.com/coaching/  
Use for the idea of accessible evidence-informed strength coaching and transparent service explanations; do not use its visual design as the aesthetic template or reuse its claims.

## Directions to Codex

1. Read `design-system.md` and the website's design skill first.
2. Inspect the five screenshot files under `design/references/`.
3. Use **Forner + Linear** for the majority of aesthetic decisions.
4. Apply **Future** to coaching explanation, imagery, and conversion UX.
5. Use **JuggernautAI** only as a cue for authentic barbell-training imagery.
6. Use **IntervalCoach** only where a real, legible, relevant data graphic aids understanding.
7. Follow the established neutral light palette and Manrope/Inter type specifications even if screenshots use other colors or fonts.
8. Do not clone the screenshots. The end result must have its own composition, copy, photography, and brand identity.
9. Capture and inspect screenshots of the actual implementation at 1440px and 375px; evaluate hierarchy, whitespace, alignment, and responsive quality.

## Download

From the repository root, run the provided `download-screenshots.sh` script with `bash path/to/download-screenshots.sh`. It writes images to `design/references/`. The script uses the source URLs listed above. If a third-party URL has expired or blocks downloads, visit the screenshot's credited page and save the screenshot manually.
